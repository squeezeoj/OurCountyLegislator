MY STORY

From the private sector to public service.

I graduated from Vestal High School and earned degrees in Computer Science and Economics from Binghamton University. I later earned a master's degree in Information Theory from SUNY Institute of Technology. My early career took me to California, where I worked in the growing software industry in the San Francisco Bay Area.

After four years, I returned to Broome County to help with my family's business, Our Country Hearts. Over the years, I've helped operate businesses serving local customers, including Our Country Hearts and Binghamton Yoga. Running a small business teaches you to listen carefully, balance competing priorities, meet a budget, and solve problems when they arise. Those lessons continue to shape my work in government.

Before joining the Legislature, my work also included software engineering, operations management, and serving as an IT director. That blend of technical and hands-on business experience taught me to look beyond how a system is supposed to work and ask how it actually performs.

“Government should be well-run, fair, and responsive to the people.”

— A belief that guides my work

Why I serve.

I became a Broome County legislator because I wanted local government to serve people better. I don't believe my job is simply to take a position and defend it. I believe it's to do the research, hear different perspectives, understand the consequences, and work toward a sensible result.

My approach is independent and practical. I care about fiscal responsibility, individual liberty, strong neighborhoods, and effective public services. I also know that very few complex problems fit neatly into partisan talking points. Respectful disagreement is useful; endless squabbling isn't.

As a legislator representing much of Endwell and portions of Endicott, I make constituent communication a priority. That includes listening to residents, examining the county budget, and asking questions about how decisions affect taxpayers and the people who depend on county services.

Work beyond the Legislature.

Community building has always been part of my life. I've helped bring neighbors together through local projects, including the Endwell Community Garden. I'm also a founding member and currently president of the Southern Tier Land Conservancy, which works to conserve the lands and waters of Broome and Tioga Counties for future generations.

My work with county health and human services has reinforced another lesson: sound financial management and quality care should support one another. At Willow Point Rehabilitation and Nursing Center and across county departments, I believe residents and employees deserve effective administration, accountability, and a commitment to improvement.

Whether I'm reviewing a county resolution, talking with a neighbor, working in a business, or walking through a local natural area, the goal is the same: leave our community stronger than I found it.
